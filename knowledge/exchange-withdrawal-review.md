# Exchange Withdrawal Review Playbook

Purpose: guide safe movement from exchange custody toward self-custody or assisted custody.

## Use When

- A client keeps material funds on an exchange.
- A client wants to withdraw quickly after market stress.
- A client is unsure whether their address verification process is safe.

## Readiness Signals

- The client can identify the destination wallet and control model.
- Withdrawal allowlists or address books are enabled where available.
- A small test withdrawal is acceptable before the full amount.
- The client has a clear rollback plan if the exchange pauses withdrawals.

## Risks

- SIM swap exposure on the exchange account.
- Unverified destination address copied from chat or email.
- Rushed withdrawal without hardware wallet confirmation.
- Confusion between network types or wrapped assets.

## Recommended Path

Use a staged withdrawal: confirm destination ownership, send a small test, verify receipt independently, then schedule the larger transfer. If the client cannot verify the destination, defer the withdrawal.
