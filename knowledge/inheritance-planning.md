# Inheritance Planning Playbook

Purpose: help clients plan successor access without exposing secret material.

## Use When

- A client has dependents, trustees, or named beneficiaries.
- A client says their family would not know how to access funds.
- A client holds Bitcoin outside a regulated custodian.

## Evidence To Collect

- Whether a legal estate plan exists.
- Who knows that the holdings exist.
- Whether successor instructions exist and where they are stored.
- Whether the custody setup needs one person or multiple people to act.

## Good Signals

- The client has named a trusted successor or fiduciary.
- Instructions explain roles and locations without revealing secrets.
- The estate attorney understands digital asset access requirements.
- Access can be tested through a non-secret walkthrough.

## Recommendation Standard

Recommend planning work before custody complexity increases. Do not recommend sharing seed words or private keys with beneficiaries.
