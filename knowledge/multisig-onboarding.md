# Multisig Onboarding Playbook

Purpose: decide whether a client is ready for multisignature custody and define the first onboarding steps.

## Use When

- A client wants to reduce single-device or single-person custody risk.
- A client has meaningful holdings and stable operational support.
- A client can tolerate a longer setup and recovery process.

## Readiness Signals

- The client understands that multiple keys and locations are involved.
- The client can name who will hold roles without giving them secret material.
- The client has a secure place for backup instructions.
- The client accepts a rehearsal before moving significant funds.

## Not Ready Signals

- The client cannot track documents reliably.
- The client wants a same-day setup for a large withdrawal.
- The client has unresolved family or business governance conflict.
- The client has not practiced single-signature hardware wallet operations.

## Recommended Path

Start with education and a dry-run ceremony. Move funds only after the client can explain recovery roles, signing quorum, and escalation contacts.
