# Hardware Wallet Handling Playbook

Purpose: establish baseline handling practices for hardware wallet setup, storage, and operational use.

## Use When

- A client is moving from custodial storage to hardware wallet custody.
- A client stores a device at home or in an office.
- A client rotates devices after a suspected compromise.

## Baseline Practices

- Purchase devices through trusted channels.
- Verify packaging and firmware through vendor-supported checks.
- Keep signing devices offline except during planned use.
- Store backup material separately from the device.

## Operational Controls

- Use a written transaction checklist for high-value movements.
- Confirm destination addresses on the device screen.
- Keep one observer present for significant transactions when practical.
- Log the date, purpose, and reviewer for each operational signing event.

## Stop Conditions

Pause the workflow if the device asks for recovery material unexpectedly, the address differs across screens, or the client cannot explain who has physical access to the device.
