# Sovereign Operations Glossary

Purpose: keep consultants and reviewers aligned on terms used in recommendations.

## Terms

- Client evidence: a direct quote, structured fact, or operational detail supplied by the client.
- Knowledge citation: an internal playbook source that supports a recommendation.
- Readiness score: a deterministic assessment derived from extracted facts, not a model confidence score.
- Stop condition: a signal that pauses an operational workflow until a reviewer resolves risk.
- Staged withdrawal: a withdrawal process that uses verification and a small test before a larger transfer.

## Usage Rules

- Use readiness score to guide workflow priority, not to approve actions automatically.
- Use knowledge citations for policy claims.
- Use client evidence for case-specific claims.
- Mark missing evidence explicitly instead of inventing it.
