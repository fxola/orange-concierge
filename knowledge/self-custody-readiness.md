# Self-Custody Readiness Playbook

Purpose: assess whether a client is ready to hold Bitcoin directly or should first improve operational controls.

## Use When

- A client wants to move funds out of a custodian or exchange.
- A client expresses concern about platform risk.
- A client asks whether a hardware wallet is enough.

## Positive Signals

- The client can describe who controls signing devices and backups.
- The client has practiced small test transactions.
- The client uses strong account security for related services.
- The client has a recovery plan that does not require remembering details from memory.

## Negative Signals

- The client has unresolved phishing or SIM swap exposure.
- The client cannot explain where backups are stored.
- The client wants to move all funds before a test transaction.
- The client has no successor access plan for meaningful holdings.

## Recommended Path

If positive signals are present, recommend a staged custody transition. If negative signals dominate, recommend education, evidence collection, and risk reduction before funds move.
